new pitches to the lines whenever the viewport moves. Row names round-trip
that movable map; intervals already use theory.interval(cantus, voice), and
part output names the same pitches. Predict interval and sound are correct,
but verify fundamental frequencies independently rather than assume it.

and bottom + 14. Remove this arbitrary modal register clamp, retaining the
audition bank's supported pitch extent and leaving species legality to the
judge. Fixed page clef: treble if initial minimum >= G3 (25), bass otherwise.

pitch/interval names and stems, plus barline) show three complete mixed Book V
bars and part of a fourth at 80, six complete and part of a seventh at 120.

measure the chosen-clef census before recommending a second staff.
