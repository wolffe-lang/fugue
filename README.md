# fugue

A cantus firmus is given on a staff drawn in the terminal. You write the voice against it, note by note, hearing
each one as you place it. The species rules of strict counterpoint are the judge: a wrong interval, a hidden
fifth, a leap left hanging, and the critic in the margin says so, by bar. Five books take you from note against
note to florid counterpoint; the last chapter gives you a subject and asks for its answer, then a countersubject,
and you have written the opening of a fugue. Every cantus is generated by rule from a seed, so no two players
see the same book, and every finished level plays.

The fourth game of the lair, the wolf games collection, on the collection's shared engine (`wolffe-lang/lair`);
the others are `pack`, `cub` and `trail`.
